## 🌿 CARE Principles for Indigenous Data

### What are the CARE Principles?

| Letter | Meaning | Description |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| **C** | Collective Benefit | Data ecosystems must enable communities to derive benefit |
| **A** | Authority to Control | Indigenous peoples' right to control data about them |
| **R** | Responsibility | Researchers work for the community, not the other way around |
| **E** | Ethics | Indigenous worldviews on ethics (not just Western IRB) |

### Why CARE > FAIR for indigenous data
- **FAIR** (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable) focuses on data characteristics
- **CARE** focuses on people and purpose
- Indigenous data sovereignty: communities own and benefit from data
- **For P0012 Yvy** — Iván MUST apply CARE + IRB together

### CARE Principles for P0012 Yvy (Indigenous territory)

| CARE principle | Action for Iván |
|----------------|-----------------|
| **Collective Benefit** | Communities get a CC-BY atlas back; results in jopara |
| **Authority to Control** | INDI + community review before any publication |
| **Responsibility** | Annual report to communities; data hosted locally |
| **Ethics** | Cultural review board (not just university IRB) |
